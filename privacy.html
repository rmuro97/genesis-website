1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Contact details: name, business name, email, phone number, and any message you submit through our contact form or by emailing us.
  • Onboarding information: if you become a client, the business information and system access you authorize us to use to build and operate your AI workflows.
  • Communications: records of your correspondence with us (email, messaging, calls).

Information collected automatically

  • Usage & device data: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referring URLs, and timestamps, collected through standard web logs and analytics.
  • Cookies: small files used to operate the site and understand usage (see Section 6).

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide, operate, and improve our services;
  • Build, configure, and run the AI workflow automations our clients engage us for;
  • Send service-related communications, proposals, and invoices;
  • Maintain the security and integrity of our systems;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Legal Bases for Processing

Where required by law, we process personal information on the basis of: your consent; the performance of a contract with you; our legitimate business interests (such as securing our services and communicating with prospects and clients); and compliance with legal obligations.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to run our business, and only with parties bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations:

  • Service providers / subprocessors: infrastructure and tooling that power our services, including Amazon Web Services (cloud hosting & AI model hosting), Anthropic (AI models), Google (workspace, sheets, and mapping/places data), and Telegram (messaging interface).
  • Professional advisors: legal, accounting, and insurance providers, where necessary.
  • Legal & safety: when required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Genesis AI, our clients, or others.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

5. Client Data We Process on Your Behalf

When we deliver services to a business client, we may process data that the client controls (such as the client’s own customer records, leads, and communications). For that data, the client is the data controller and Genesis AI acts as a service provider / processor. We use that data only to provide the contracted services, under the terms of our written agreement, and we do not use it for any unrelated purpose. Specific data-handling terms are set out in each client’s Services Agreement.

6. Cookies & Analytics

We use essential cookies to operate the site and may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use it. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect site functionality.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, to provide our services, and to meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. When no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it. Client data is retained and deleted according to each client’s Services Agreement.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse, including encrypted connections, access controls, and reputable cloud providers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to opt out of certain processing, and to withdraw consent. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information (we do not sell personal information). To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International Users

We are based in the United States, and your information may be processed in the U.S. and other countries where our service providers operate. By using our services, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

12. Changes to This Policy
